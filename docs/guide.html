Setup & Pairing

How to Connect ClipSync

Discover peers effortlessly on your local Wi-Fi network and verify real-time clipboard sync between desktops and mobile devices.

1

Connect to Same Wi-Fi

Ensure your computer (Windows, macOS, Linux) and phone or secondary laptop are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or local subnet.

2

Launch ClipSync

Open ClipSync on both devices. The background engine immediately initiates Zeroconf mDNS peer discovery on UDP ports 5353 and 9999.

3

Instant Synchronization

Copy any text snippet on Device A. It arrives in Device B's clipboard history feed in under 10 milliseconds, ready for immediate use.

Desktop Pairing & Workflow (Windows / macOS / Linux)

ClipSync runs as a native lightweight desktop client with an integrated system tray daemon. When you open the window, you have two core operational views:

Desktop GUI — Devices Tab
1. Discovered Peers View

Lists all actively discovered machines on your local network. A glowing green indicator confirms bidirectional heartbeats.

assets/screenshots/devices.png
Desktop GUI — Clipboard Tab
2. Live Clipboard Cards & Pinning

Every copied snippet appears here in real time. Pinned snippets are highlighted in warm amber and persist across app restarts.

assets/screenshots/clipboard.png

💡 Desktop Pro-Tips:

  • Closing the desktop window minimizes ClipSync to your System Tray / Menu Bar, keeping sync active in the background.
  • Right-click the tray icon to quickly bring the window back to front or select Quit to exit.
  • On Linux, ensure wl-clipboard (Wayland) or xclip (X11) is installed so clipboard events trigger reliably.

Android Mobile Pairing & Tap-to-Copy

ClipSync Android provides an intuitive touch interface. In compliance with Android 10+ privacy protections, ClipSync provides a seamless Tap-to-Copy workflow:

Android App — Discovered Devices
1. Mobile Radar & Active Peers

When you launch ClipSync on Android, it browses _clipsync._tcp to automatically list your connected PC workstations.

assets/screenshots/android-devices.png
Android App — Clipboard History
2. Tap-to-Copy Cards

Clips copied on your computer arrive in your phone feed. Simply tap COPY on any card to paste it anywhere on mobile.

assets/screenshots/android-clipboard.png

📱 Battery Optimization Setting:

To keep ClipSync's incoming UDP listener active when your phone screen is off, set ClipSync's battery usage to Unrestricted in Settings > Apps > ClipSync > Battery.
